About us

Northgate Barbers has been cutting hair on Market Street since 2012. We are a small, friendly shop that believes a good haircut takes time, so we never rush a customer and we never double-book a chair.

Whether you want a sharp skin fade, a traditional short back and sides, or a relaxing hot towel shave, you will get an honest chat, a clean finish and a fair price.

Walk-ins are welcome when there is space, but booking online guarantees your slot.
